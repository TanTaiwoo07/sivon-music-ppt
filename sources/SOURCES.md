## 1. GEMA v. Suno —— 慕尼黑地区法院判决

- **一手**：GEMA 官方新闻稿，2026-07-31
  <https://www.gema.de/en/w/gema-prevails-over-suno>

  - 案号：42 O 763/25

## 3. Deezer 数据

- **一手**：Jesper Wendel, *Deezer: AI music has surpassed 50% of new music uploads for the first time*, Deezer Newsroom, **2026-07-21**
  <https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/>
