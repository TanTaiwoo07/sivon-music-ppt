## 四、Speaker B 英文讲稿：三个问题

**PPT：** Page 6–9

> Thank you, A. The AI music wave looks fun. But there are three problems.
>
> **Problem one: copyright.**AI music is trained on many copyrighted songs. Many platforms did not get permission.A German court ruled that **Suno infringed copyright**. Universal, Sony, and Warner also sued Suno and Udio.Sivon Music sounds like a joke. But every old song comes from someone’s work.
>
> **Problem two: homogenization.**AI likes “safe” and familiar styles.One producer said AI songs are like a **common face**: neat, acceptable, but forgettable.Sivon Music is also a template. Same style, different old songs.
>
> **Problem three: who is replaced?**AI is not replacing all creation.It is replacing **low-skill, mass-produced music work**: short-video music, ad music, simple tracks.Some songwriters lost work.But **mixing engineers are more valued**, because mixing needs human judgment and taste.
>
> So the real question is not “Can AI make music?”The real question is: **What is it made from? What does it replace? What does it leave to humans?**
>
> Now Speaker C will talk about the essence and what we can do.

## 五、Speaker C 英文讲稿：实质 + 行动 + 升华

**PPT：** Page 10–14

Thank you, B. So what is the essence?

I think it is **fast production vs. human feeling**.AI can make perfect music.But it has **no real feelings**.A song moves us because a person felt something.AI can learn to “sing.”But only people can “feel.”

This is also a **social change**.We need to ask: who owns creativity? Who gets paid? What do we value?

As young people, and as CS students, what should we do?

**First, use AI, but keep human control.**Do not just type one sentence and accept the result.Decide what you want to say first.AI gives options. You make decisions.

**Second, don’t spread bad AI covers.**If an AI cover uses someone’s voice without permission, do not like it. Do not share it.Your clicks are votes.

**Third, as CS students, understand the technology and speak up.**We know how AI works.We should ask: Where does the training data come from? Is the output too close to the original?People who understand technology should point out problems.

So let’s go back to Sivon Music.It is funny. It makes us laugh.But after laughing, we can ask one question:**If AI can turn any old song into electronic music, what new songs do we still want to hear?**

AI cannot answer that.Only people can.

Thank you. We are happy to answer your questions.

## 六、Q&A 简单英文

**Q1: What is Sivon Music?**

> It is AI covers of old songs. Deep AI voice, strong electronic beats. The Sivon version of *Yu Ai* is famous.

**Q2: What does the Suno case mean?**

> It means training AI with copyrighted music without permission can be illegal. The output can also be a problem.

**Q3: Will AI replace musicians?**

> It will replace low-skill, mass-produced work. But it cannot replace human judgment and taste.

**Q4: What can students do now?**

> Use AI with human control. Don’t spread bad AI covers. As CS students, understand the technology and speak up.
